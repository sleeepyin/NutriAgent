Offline logged-horizon force-answer. States stay the logged weak-step trajectory; the policy does not execute tools. The decision step is the first predicted STOP or ABSTAIN, or the last logged step if the policy never predicts a terminal action. Force-answer energy is the latest finite nutri_dish.energy_kcal at or before that step. Later logged calories are not credited. Dishes with no finite total are unscored and not imputed. Error is absolute kcal versus the cafe dish label passed in by the caller. Full-set MAE averages that error on scored dishes and keeps policy ABSTAIN decisions in the average. Coverage and Selective MAE keep the dishes whose decision action is not ABSTAIN (vdr.eval.selective, flag rule). AURC uses the caller-supplied abstain_score at the decision step (higher means less confident). The score may be softmax P(ABSTAIN) or a calibrated score recorded by the caller. Ties keep sorted dish_id order. The primary population is a logged STOP with status pass or corrected and a finite terminal energy. A sensitivity population also scores logged ABSTAIN trajectories when an earlier step has a finite total. A secondary score cut at 0.5 is reported and was not fit. This is not an on-policy rollout and not an rgb_test VLM evaluation.
