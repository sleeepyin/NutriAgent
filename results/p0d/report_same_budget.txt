P0d same-budget evaluation
definition: p0d_same_budget_overhead_force_answer
Same-budget full set on official rgb_test dishes that have an overhead rgb.png. Each method commits one energy and one mass. The decision step is the first predicted STOP or ABSTAIN, or the last step if the method never predicts a terminal action. Committed nutrition is the latest finite nutri_dish at or before that step. Later totals are not credited. A dish missing energy or mass is unscored and is not imputed as zero. The paired table keeps only dishes scored by every method. Cafe labels score the error and do not choose actions, codes, grams, or thresholds. Tool cost counts RETRIEVE, CORRECT_CODE, RE_PORTION, and RE_DECOMPOSE through the decision step. STOP and ABSTAIN are not tools. The step cap is K=32. Coverage and AURC are auxiliary. This is not the hold-out action-agreement table and not a reinforcement-learning run.

Pool: official rgb_test 709 ids, 507 with an overhead rgb.png. Ids without an image are not imputed.
Budget K=32. Tool cost counts CORRECT_CODE, RETRIEVE, RE_DECOMPOSE, RE_PORTION. ABSTAIN and STOP are not tools. The Q/Cost weights are the selector's denominator, not this count.
Cafe labels score error only. No threshold was fit on rgb_test.
Coverage and AURC are auxiliary and were not used to pick a method.
This is not the hold-out action-agreement table.
